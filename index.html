Advanced Cath Lab Suite & Imaging Guidance

Equipped with state-of-the-art Digital Subtraction Angiography (DSA), ultra-low dose pulsed fluoroscopy, and real-time color Doppler ultrasound for sub-millimeter catheter navigation.

VASCULAR CATH LAB

Digital Subtraction Angiography (DSA)

DSA digitally "subtracts" bones and surrounding soft tissues from X-ray imaging, providing a crystal-clear, magnified roadmap of only blood vessels. This allows Dr. Rohan to steer microcatheters as thin as 1 mm into target vessels feeding fibroids, tumors, or the prostate with sub-millimeter precision.

Used In: Uterine Fibroids (UFE), Prostate (PAE), Knee Pain (GAE), Liver TACE, and Angioplasty.
REAL-TIME MAPPING

High-Resolution Ultrasound & Color Doppler

Live zero-radiation ultrasound allows visualization of moving blood cells and vascular valves. Dr. Rohan performs every needle puncture with live visual tracking, eliminating "blind" attempts, minimizing tissue irritation, and ensuring 100% first-pass accuracy.

Used In: Varicose vein EVLA laser, Thyroid RFA ablation, Chemoport placement, and precision biopsies.
CROSS-SECTIONAL ACCURACY

CT-Guided Stereotactic Navigation

Multi-detector CT scanning provides true 3D cross-sectional views of deep internal organs. Dr. Rohan can guide needle antennas directly into deep liver, lung, or spinal lesions while avoiding vital blood vessels and nerves by millimeters.

Used In: Deep tumor microwave ablation, lung nodule biopsies, and complex abdominal pigtail drainage.
TUMOR & VEIN ABLATION

Radiofrequency & Microwave Ablation Generators

Advanced generators deliver carefully calibrated electromagnetic thermal energy through hair-thin probe tips. Thermal energy coagulates diseased nodular tissue or diseased vein walls in minutes, with zero surgical incision or scarring.

Used In: Thyroid nodule shrinkage, liver cancer ablation, and varicose vein obliteration.
