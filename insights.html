CLINICAL EXPLAINER

What is Interventional Radiology (IR)?

Interventional Radiology is a medical specialty that uses medical imaging guidance (such as Fluoroscopy X-ray, Ultrasound, and CT) to perform targeted minimally invasive procedures. Rather than opening the body with a large scalpel incision, an Interventional Radiologist navigates micro-instruments through a 2mm puncture directly to the diseased site.

PATIENT CHECKLIST

What to Bring to Your First Consultation

To make the most of your consultation with Dr. Rohan in Varanasi, please organize the following medical records:

  1. Original Radiology Scans: Film sheets or DICOM CDs of Ultrasounds, Doppler studies, CT scans, or MRIs.
  2. Recent Blood Reports: CBC, Kidney Function Test (Creatinine/BUN), Coagulation profile (PT/INR).
  3. Current Prescription List: All daily medications, especially blood thinners (Aspirin, Clopidogrel, Warfarin).
  4. Doctor Referral Letters: Prior surgical notes or physician discharge summaries if applicable.
FINANCIAL & TPA

Health Insurance & Cashless Mediclaim

Under modern insurance guidelines by IRDAI, advanced daycare interventional radiology procedures that do not require 24-hour hospitalization are fully eligible for Mediclaim and Cashless TPA approvals.

  • Varicose Vein EVLA / Laser Ablation is widely covered under vascular surgery daycare clauses.
  • Uterine Fibroid Embolization (UFE) is eligible as an approved alternative to hysterectomy.
  • Oncology procedures (TACE, Chemoports) and Dialysis catheters are covered under critical illness and hospitalization benefits.
  • Our billing desk helps coordinate pre-authorization documents with all major TPAs.
