Cath Lab Facility • Sigra, Varanasi

Cath Lab & Advanced Technology Suite

Our specialized Daycare Cath Lab in Varanasi features Digital Subtraction Angiography (DSA), ultra-low dose pulsed fluoroscopy, and real-time color Doppler ultrasound for sub-millimeter catheter navigation with zero surgical incisions.

VASCULAR CATH LAB

Digital Subtraction Angiography (DSA)

DSA digitally "subtracts" bones and surrounding soft tissues from X-ray images, providing an unobstructed view of only blood vessels. This allows Dr. Rohan to navigate microcatheters into arteries as narrow as 1 millimeter with extraordinary precision.

Used in: Uterine Fibroid Embolization (UFE), Prostate (PAE), Knee (GAE), TACE, and Angioplasty.
REAL-TIME MAPPING

High-Resolution Ultrasound & Color Doppler

Provides zero-radiation, live-action visualization of blood flow, tissue layers, and vein valves. Guides every pinhole puncture directly into veins without "blind" stabbing, ensuring 100% first-pass accuracy and patient comfort.

Used in: Varicose vein EVLA, Thyroid RFA, Chemoport placement, and FNAC Biopsy.
CROSS-SECTIONAL PRECISION

CT-Guided Stereotactic Navigation

Multi-detector CT guidance creates 3D cross-sectional slices of deep internal organs (such as lungs, liver, and spine). Enables millimeter-exact needle navigation deep inside the body while safely avoiding vital arteries and nerves.

Used in: Deep tumor microwave ablation, lung nodule biopsy, and drainage of complex abdominal abscesses.
THERMAL DESTRUCTION

Radiofrequency & Microwave Ablation (RFA/MWA)

State-of-the-art generators deliver carefully calibrated electromagnetic energy through fine needle electrodes. The thermal zone coagulates diseased cells (tumors or enlarged nodules) within minutes, sparing adjacent healthy tissue.

Used in: Thyroid nodule shrinkage, liver cancer ablation, and varicose vein obliteration.
Patient Safety Standards

Ultra-Low Dose Radiation & Strict Infection Control

We strictly adhere to the ALARA (As Low As Reasonably Achievable) radiation protocol, using digital dose reduction filters and pulsed fluoroscopy to ensure maximum safety.

SAFETY 01

Pulsed Fluoroscopy

Reduces cumulative X-ray exposure by up to 70% compared to conventional continuous fluoroscopy units.

SAFETY 02

HEPA-Filtered Cath Suite

Our procedure rooms maintain positive pressure laminar airflow, exceeding international sterile surgical standards.

SAFETY 03

Single-Use Microcatheters

All microcatheters, guidewires, and embolic delivery systems are 100% disposable and never re-sterilized.

SAFETY 04

Non-Ionic Contrast Media

We utilize premium hypo-osmolar non-ionic contrast agents to safeguard kidney function during angiography.
