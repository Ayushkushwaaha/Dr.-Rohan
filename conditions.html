Vascular Health Details ↗

Varicose Veins & Venous Ulcers

Swollen, tortuous leg veins caused by failing one-way valves, resulting in pooling venous reflux, leg heaviness, and chronic stasis skin ulcers.

Heavy, aching legs Ankle swelling Night calf cramps Dark skin pigmentation Non-healing leg ulcers
Pinhole IR Solution: Laser (EVLA), Radiofrequency, or VenaSeal Glue. 30-min daycare closure.

Women's Health Details ↗

Uterine Fibroids & Adenomyosis

Benign muscular tumors inside the uterus causing excessive menstrual bleeding, severe anemia, pelvic fullness, and intense periodic cramping.

Excessive menstrual bleeding Passing large clots Severe period cramps Pelvic pressure/fullness Frequent urination
Pinhole IR Solution: Uterine Fibroid Embolization (UFE). Shrinks fibroids, saves the uterus.
View Full Disease Breakdown & IR Cure →
Men's Health & Urology Details ↗

Enlarged Prostate (BPH)

Non-cancerous enlargement of the prostate gland in men aged 50+, pinching the urethra and obstructing normal bladder outflow.

Weak urine stream Waking multiple times at night Difficulty starting urination Urinary retention / catheter Urgency & dribbling
Pinhole IR Solution: Prostate Artery Embolization (PAE). No surgery via urethra, zero sexual side-effects.
View Full Disease Breakdown & IR Cure →
Joints & Pain Care Details ↗

Knee Osteoarthritis & Joint Pain

Degenerative cartilage wear accompanied by chronic inflammatory neo-vessel proliferation fueling persistent, aching knee joint pain.

Pain walking or climbing stairs Morning knee joint stiffness Swelling around the knee Failed pain medicines/gels Desire to avoid knee surgery
Pinhole IR Solution: Genicular Artery Embolization (GAE). Blocks pain vessels, relieves stiffness.
View Full Disease Breakdown & IR Cure →
Endocrine & Neck Details ↗

Thyroid Nodules & Neck Goitres

Benign lumps or cysts in the thyroid gland causing visible neck bulging, throat constriction, difficulty swallowing solids, and cosmetic anxiety.

Visible lump in lower neck Difficulty swallowing solids Feeling of throat constriction Cosmetic distress Desire to avoid neck scar
Pinhole IR Solution: Thyroid Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA). Scarless needle shrinkage in 30 mins.
View Full Disease Breakdown & IR Cure →
Men's Fertility & Urology Details ↗

Varicocele & Male Infertility

Abnormal dilation of scrotal veins raising testicular temperature, causing chronic dragging pain, and impairing sperm count and motility.

Dull aching scrotal pain Worse after prolonged standing Abnormal semen parameters Low sperm count or motility Visible 'bag of worms' veins
Pinhole IR Solution: Varicocele Embolization. Catheter coil/glue blockage with zero groin incisions.
View Full Disease Breakdown & IR Cure →
Vascular Emergency Details ↗

Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT)

Sudden blood clot formation in deep leg or pelvic veins, carrying high risks of fatal pulmonary embolism if clots travel to the lungs.

Sudden severe leg swelling Throbbing calf pain Warmth and reddish skin Risk of pulmonary embolism Engorged superficial veins
Pinhole IR Solution: Catheter-Directed Thrombolysis (CDT) & IVC Filter placement for clot safety.
View Full Disease Breakdown & IR Cure →
Vascular Health & PAD Details ↗

Diabetic Foot & Arterial Blockages

Atherosclerotic plaques narrow leg arteries, severely restricting oxygen supply and causing gangrene, night foot pain, and non-healing ulcers.

Calf cramps while walking Foot pain keeping you awake Cold, pale feet or toes Non-healing diabetic ulcers Limb amputation threat
Pinhole IR Solution: Peripheral Angioplasty & Stenting. Restores blood flow and saves the leg.
View Full Disease Breakdown & IR Cure →
Oncology & Liver Details ↗

Liver Tumors (HCC & Metastases)

Primary or secondary malignant growths in the liver that cannot be surgically cut out due to tumor size, position, or underlying cirrhosis.

Unexplained weight loss Right upper abdominal ache Elevated AFP tumor marker Inoperable liver mass on scan Chronic cancer fatigue
Pinhole IR Solution: TACE / TARE and Microwave Ablation. Delivers targeted chemo and kills tumor cells.
View Full Disease Breakdown & IR Cure →
Neuro-Intervention Details ↗

Brain Aneurysm & Acute Stroke

Critical ballooning of cerebral vessels risking catastrophic rupture, acute clot stroke causing sudden paralysis, and carotid artery narrowing.

Sudden explosive headache Facial drooping / weakness Slurred speech / FAST signs Carotid neck artery stenosis TIA mini-strokes
Pinhole IR Solution: Mechanical Thrombectomy, Aneurysm Coiling, Flow Diverters & Carotid Stenting.
View Full Disease Breakdown & IR Cure →
Women's Health Details ↗

Pelvic Congestion Syndrome (PCS)

Incompetent ovarian and pelvic veins causing blood to pool backward, producing deep pelvic varicosities and chronic, unexplained daily pain.

Chronic pelvic pain >6 months Pain worse after standing Pain during intercourse Heavy, painful periods Thigh/perineal varicose veins
Pinhole IR Solution: Ovarian Vein Embolization. Catheter coil closure of refluxing pelvic veins in daycare.
View Full Disease Breakdown & IR Cure →
Vascular & Dialysis Access Details ↗

Dialysis AV Fistula Dysfunction

Narrowing (stenosis) or blood clots obstructing the patient's arteriovenous dialysis fistula, causing high pressure alarms and arm swelling.

Low blood flow on dialysis High venous pressure alarms Loss of fistula thrill/hum Severe arm or face swelling Prolonged needle bleeding
Pinhole IR Solution: Pinhole Fistuloplasty (Balloon Dilation) & Catheter Declotting to salvage the fistula.
