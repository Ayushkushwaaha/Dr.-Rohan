Specialized Interventional Categories

Interventional Radiology covers 9 comprehensive clinical disciplines. Discover how image-guided pinhole techniques offer surgical alternatives across vascular, gynecological, urological, oncological, and neurological conditions.

🩸
SPECIALTY 01

Peripheral Vascular

Advanced catheter and laser solutions for venous and arterial disorders of the extremities. Minimizes pain, prevents amputations, and restores normal circulation.

  • Varicose Veins EVLA (Endovenous Laser)
  • VenaSeal Medical Glue & Radiofrequency (RFA)
  • Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT) Catheter Thrombolysis
  • Diabetic Foot & Critical Limb Ischemia Angioplasty
  • Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD) Stenting & Atherectomy
Explore Vascular Care →
🌸
SPECIALTY 02

Obstetrics & Gynecology

Organ-preserving, uterus-saving pinhole therapies for benign gynecological conditions. Proven non-surgical alternatives to hysterectomy.

  • Uterine Fibroid Embolization (UFE / UAE)
  • Adenomyosis Embolization
  • Pelvic Congestion Syndrome (Ovarian Vein Embolization)
  • Post-Partum Hemorrhage (PPH Emergency Embolization)
  • Fallopian Tube Recanalization for Infertility
Explore Women's Health →
💧
SPECIALTY 03

Urological Interventions

Endovascular and percutaneous treatments for prostate enlargement, scrotal varicoceles, and renal urinary tract obstructions without urethral surgery.

  • Prostate Artery Embolization (PAE for BPH)
  • Varicocele Embolization (Fertility & Pain Relief)
  • Renal Angiomyolipoma (AML) Embolization
  • Percutaneous Nephrostomy (PCN) & Stenting
  • Hemodialysis Tunnelled Permacath Placement
Explore Urology IR →
🦴
SPECIALTY 04

Musculoskeletal & Joints

Non-surgical embolization targeting abnormal hypervascularity and chronic inflammatory pain nerves in joints, spine, and bones.

  • Genicular Artery Embolization (GAE) for Knee Arthritis
  • Adhesive Capsulitis (Frozen Shoulder) Embolization
  • Vertebroplasty / Kyphoplasty for Spine Fractures
  • Osteoid Osteoma Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA)
  • Image-Guided Facet & Joint Pain Injections
Explore Joint Pain IR →
🌿
SPECIALTY 05

Gastrointestinal & Liver

Minimally invasive interventional oncology and life-saving bleeding control for liver, biliary system, stomach, and bowel disorders.

  • TACE (Transarterial Chemoembolization) for Liver Tumors
  • TARE (Yttrium-90 Radioembolization)
  • Microwave / RFA Liver Tumor Ablation
  • Acute GI Bleed Embolization (Ulcers / Diverticula)
  • PTBD (Percutaneous Biliary Drainage) & Biliary Stents
Explore GI & Liver IR →
👤
SPECIALTY 06

Head & Neck

Thermal ablation and endovascular embolization for thyroid lumps, severe nasal hemorrhages, and vascular malformations without neck incisions.

  • Thyroid Nodule Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA)
  • Thyroid Microwave Ablation (Zero Neck Scar)
  • Carotid Artery Stenting (CAS for Stroke Prevention)
  • Severe Epistaxis (Refractory Nosebleed) Embolization
  • Facial & Neck Vascular Malformation Sclerotherapy
Explore Head & Neck →
🫁
SPECIALTY 07

Thoracic (Chest & Lungs)

Image-guided procedures for chronic coughing of blood, thoracic fluid management, lung lesions, and long-term chemotherapy access lines.

  • Bronchial Artery Embolization (Hemoptysis Bleed Control)
  • Chemoport Insertion for Long-Term Chemotherapy
  • PICC Line Insertion (Ultrasound-Guided)
  • CT-Guided Precision Lung Biopsy
  • Pleural Effusion Drainage & Pigtail Insertion
Explore Thoracic IR →
🧠
SPECIALTY 08

Neuro Interventions

Endovascular microcatheter treatments for brain aneurysms, acute ischemic stroke clots, and complex intracranial vascular diseases.

  • Emergency Mechanical Thrombectomy for Acute Stroke
  • Brain Aneurysm Coiling & Flow Diverter Stenting
  • Brain Arteriovenous Malformation (AVM) Embolization
  • Carotid Cavernous Fistula (CCF) Embolization
  • Diagnostic Cerebral Digital Subtraction Angiography (DSA)
Explore Neuro Care →
🔬
SPECIALTY 09

Basic & Daycare Interventions

Essential outpatient diagnostic biopsies and therapeutic drainage procedures performed under ultrasound or CT guidance in 20–30 minutes.

  • USG & CT-Guided Core Biopsy (Liver, Lung, Kidney, Nodes)
  • Fine Needle Aspiration Cytology (FNAC)
  • Pigtail Catheter Insertion for Abscess Drainage
  • Therapeutic Ascitic & Pleural Fluid Paracentesis
  • AV Fistula Salvage Balloon Angioplasty
Explore Daycare IR →
