      Vascular & Venous Care

      Varicose Veins: Endovenous Laser (EVLA) & VenaSeal Glue

      Endovenous Laser (EVLA) Radiofrequency (RFA) VenaSeal Medical Glue Foam Sclerotherapy

      Varicose veins occur when faulty valves cause blood to pool in leg veins, leading to pain, swelling, heaviness, discoloration, and non-healing venous ulcers. Traditional vein stripping surgery required painful cuts and long hospital stays.

      • Laser & RFA Thermal Ablation: Under ultrasound guidance, a hair-thin optical fiber gently seals diseased veins from the inside.
      • VenaSeal Glue: A proprietary medical adhesive seals the vein without any heat or multiple tumescent needle pricks.
      • Walk-In, Walk-Out: Entirely performed under local numbing in 30–45 minutes. Walk home immediately with zero stitches.

      Women's Health

      Uterine Fibroid Embolization (UFE / UAE)

      Uterine Fibroids Adenomyosis Heavy Menstrual Bleeding Pelvic Pain Relief

      Uterine Fibroids are benign muscular tumors in the uterus causing severe bleeding, anemia, and intense pelvic pressure. Women are often told hysterectomy (uterus removal) is their only choice—this is no longer true.

      • How UFE Works: A microcatheter is guided via a tiny wrist or groin pinhole into the uterine arteries. Microscopic embolic particles block the fibroid's blood supply.
      • Fibroid Shrinkage: Deprived of blood, fibroids shrink by 60–80% and soften, stopping abnormal bleeding within 1–2 cycles.
      • Uterus Intact: The normal uterus remains completely healthy. Avoids surgical menopause, surgical adhesions, and long bed rest.
      Men's Health & Urology

      Prostate Artery Embolization (PAE for BPH)

      Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH) Enlarged Prostate Preserves Sexual Function No Penile Surgery

      Men over 50 frequently suffer from urinary urgency, weak stream, frequent nighttime urination, and retention caused by an enlarged prostate gland. Traditional TURP surgery cuts through the urethra, carrying risks of retrograde ejaculation and incontinence.

      • Catheter-Directed Pinhole Relief: Dr. Rohan navigates microscopic catheters from the wrist artery down to the tiny vessels feeding the prostate.
      • Preserves Sexual Function: Because the urethra and nerves are never touched or cut, PAE eliminates the risk of erectile dysfunction or retrograde ejaculation.
      • Discharge in 24 Hours: Patients experience noticeable improvement in urinary flow within 1 to 2 weeks as the prostate gently shrinks.

      Musculoskeletal & Pain Care

      Genicular Artery Embolization (GAE) for Knee Pain

      Knee Osteoarthritis Chronic Joint Inflammation Non-Surgical Relief Mobility Restoration

      For patients suffering from mild-to-moderate knee osteoarthritis who haven't responded to physiotherapy or joint injections, GAE offers a groundbreaking, non-surgical pathway to lasting pain relief.

      • Targeting Chronic Inflammation: Osteoarthritis stimulates abnormal new micro-vessels (hypervascularity) and sensory nerve sprouting that drive chronic knee pain.
      • Precision Embolization: Under X-ray guidance, micro-particles block these abnormal inflammatory vessels while preserving healthy knee circulation.
      • Significant Pain Reduction: Up to 80% of patients report dramatic pain reduction and improved walking distance within 2 weeks.
      Endocrine & Neck

      Thyroid Nodule Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA)

      Benign Thyroid Nodules No Neck Scars Preserves Thyroid Hormones 30-Minute Outpatient

      Benign thyroid nodules can grow large, causing cosmetic neck lumps, difficulty swallowing, or a choking feeling. Traditional surgery removes part or all of the thyroid gland, leaving a prominent neck scar and requiring lifelong daily hormone replacement pills.

      • Scarless Needle Puncture: An ultra-fine specialized electrode is guided directly into the nodule under high-resolution real-time ultrasound.
      • Thermal Shrinkage: Radiofrequency energy precisely heats and coagulates the nodule tissue, causing it to shrink by 50–90% over time.
      • No Lifelong Pills: The surrounding healthy thyroid gland is untouched, so patients maintain their natural hormone production.

      Interventional Oncology

      TACE, TARE & Tumor Ablation

      Liver Cancer (HCC / Metastases) TACE (Chemoembolization) TARE (Yttrium-90) Microwave / Cryoablation

      For tumors that are inoperable or for patients unsuitable for extensive open resection, Interventional Oncology provides targeted local tumor destruction with minimal collateral damage to normal organs.

      • TACE: Super-selective catheters deliver high-dose chemo drugs directly inside the tumor's feeding vessels, then seal them to starve the tumor.
      • Microwave Ablation: Needle antennas inserted directly into tumors in the liver, lung, kidney, or bone destroy malignant cells with pinpoint heat.
      • Minimal Systemic Toxicity: Because chemo is concentrated locally, nausea, hair loss, and systemic weakness are significantly minimized.
      Vascular Access & Dialysis Support

      Chemoports, PICC Lines & Dialysis Access

      Chemoport Insertion PICC Line Placement Permacath (Tunnelled Line) AV Fistula Angioplasty

      Safe, reliable vascular access is a lifeline for cancer patients undergoing chemotherapy and chronic kidney disease patients needing hemodialysis.

      • Chemoport Insertion: A small titanium port placed under the chest skin prevents painful repeated vein punctures during cancer treatment.
      • Permacath & Hickman Lines: Long-term tunnelled dialysis catheters placed under real-time ultrasound and fluoroscopic guidance ensure maximum blood flow and zero line kinking.
      • AV Fistula Salvage: Image-guided balloon angioplasty opens up clotted or narrowed dialysis fistulas, rescuing them without surgical remake.
